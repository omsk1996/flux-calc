Flux Calc Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Flux Calc is a calculator app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. Flux Calc does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or usage data. The app has no account system, no advertising, no analytics, and does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

Your calculation history and settings (such as degree or radian mode) are saved only on your device so they are there the next time you open the app. You can clear the history at any time from inside the app, or remove all app data by uninstalling it.

If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include this local data in your own device backup, which is controlled by you and your Google account. The developer never has access to it.

Clipboard

When you long-press a result, the app copies that result to your clipboard. The app does not read your clipboard.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
